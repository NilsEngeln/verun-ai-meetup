# Brand — VERUN Storybook

_Status: active_

VERUN should feel precise, calm, institutional, and quietly futuristic.

- Primary: deep forest green
- Accent: vivid VERUN green
- Neutrals: cool off-white, graphite, steel grey
- Typography: clear European grotesk/system sans; compact mono for system labels
- Voice: concise, specific, confident; avoid hype and unqualified compliance claims
- Visual language: architectural systems, mapped pathways, controlled signals, visible handoffs
