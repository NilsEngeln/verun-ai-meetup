# Brand — VERUN AI Meetup Explorer

_Status: active · Source of truth: `ERSTER × VERUN branding brief`, 2026-07 v1.0_

VERUN is institutional, agentic fintech. Its voice is precise, technical, calm,
compliance-first, and source-backed. The product subject is the AI agent acting
for an investor. Avoid Web3 hype, vague superlatives, and unsourced claims.

## Canonical tokens

- Primary green: `#16a34a`; hover/pressed: `#15803d`
- Green support: `#f0fdf4`, `#dcfce7`, `#bbf7d0`, `#86efac`, `#22c55e`
- Heading ink: `#0a2540`
- Body text: `#4a5b73`
- Meta text: `#8898aa`
- Backgrounds: `#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`; highlighted cards: `#f0fdf4`
- Borders: `#e5ebf7`; dividers: `#eef2f7`
- Semantic red: `#dc2626`, `#fef2f2`, `#fecaca`

Purple, teal, blue, coral, gradients, and arbitrary greys are not part of the
brand system. Use Inter for all interface copy. JetBrains Mono is reserved for
code snippets and compact system metadata.

## Interface rules

- Headings use navy and weight 800; body copy stays grey.
- Primary actions are solid green pills. Secondary actions are white pills with
  a canonical border.
- Cards use white fill, canonical borders, 16px radius, and the documented
  shadow scale.
- Layout spacing follows the 4px grid.
- Architectural visuals should show visible handoffs and controlled systems,
  while remaining calm and readable.
